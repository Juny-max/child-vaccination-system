# Quick Questions for Nurse Aunty - CHW & Catchment Areas

**Just 3 simple questions to help us build the system correctly! 🙏**

---

## Question 1: How CHWs Know Which Children to Visit

**Aunty, imagine this situation:**

Sister Ama is a CHW working in Adabraka community. She needs to go do home visits for children who need vaccines.

Now, there's a baby called Kojo. Kojo's mother went to Ridge Hospital to give birth and registered the baby there. But the family lives in Adabraka.

**My question is:** 
- Should Sister Ama visit baby Kojo at home in Adabraka? 
- Or should someone from Ridge Hospital area visit him instead?

**In simple terms:** When you assign a CHW to follow up a child at home, do you look at where the child **lives** or where the mother **registered** at the hospital?

## Question 2: When Children Don't Live with Mother

**Aunty, you know how sometimes babies don't stay with their mothers?**

Like these situations you see at the clinic:
- Baby stays with grandmother at Osu every day, but the mother lives in Teshie
- Parents separated - child is with father's mother in Madina
- Little girl stays with aunty during school days, only goes to mother on weekends

**My questions are:** 
- When this happens, which address do you write in the child's records?
- For the CHW home visits, which house should they go to - grandmother's house or mother's house?
- Do you see this kind of thing often at the clinic?

## Question 3: When Families Move to Different Towns

**Aunty, imagine this:**

A mother registered her baby at this hospital. The CHW Sister Ama has been visiting them at home in Adabraka. Everything is going well.

Then one day, the family moves to Kumasi. The mother continues the vaccinations at a hospital in Kumasi.

**My questions are:**
- At the new hospital in Kumasi, can they see the old vaccination records from here?
- Does the mother need to register the child again as a new patient?
- What happens to Sister Ama's records - does she know the family moved?
- How does the CHW in Kumasi know about this child who just moved there?

**Basically:** When families move towns, how do the records follow them?
